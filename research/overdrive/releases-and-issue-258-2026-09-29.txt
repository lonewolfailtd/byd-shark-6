### ✨ New Features

- **Glass Widgets (opt-in)** — Glass instrument widgets over the home screen or every app: battery & range, charging, live trip, RoadSense, vehicle power & tyres, and vehicle controls. Turn them on from the new Widgets page; when off, nothing runs.
- **Your layout** — Choose which widgets show and their order, position, size and tint. Charging can show only while charging.
- **Power & tyres at a glance** — Power flows from the battery to the motors and back on regen, the wheels show tyre pressure, and an optional blind-spot view lights the side where the radar sees a vehicle.
- **Controls from the overlay** — Lock, climate, boot, lights and all four windows (close, vent, open), in Park only.
- **BYD Shark 6 support (opt-in)** — The Shark's DiLink 5.0 firmware locks the folder Overdrive's daemons use, so nothing could start. A new **Diagnostics → Daemon storage** toggle moves that folder to one the head unit allows, and the Shark is recognised from its firmware so the camera order (8,9,5,4) is picked automatically. Other vehicles are unaffected unless the toggle is turned on.

## 🇴🇩 Overdrive Update v48.0: Camera Reliability, Telemetry & Projection
## [Update v48.18]: DiLink 3.0 & 5.0 Fixes, Tailscale HTTPS

### ✨ New Features
- **Optional DiLink 5 parked cloud keep-alive** — While the car is off, Overdrive can poll BYD Cloud about once per minute to help prevent deep sleep and preserve ADB/camera access. It requires a verified BYD Cloud account and internet, may use vehicle SIM data and extra parked battery, follows the active proxy route, and stops on ACC-on or daemon shutdown.
---

### ✨ New Features

- **Optional decoupled camera pipeline** — On non-DiLink 4/5 vehicles, enable **Decoupled encoder pipeline** under **Diagnostics → Camera Mapping**, then restart the camera daemon or cycle ACC. It keeps the BYD AVM/reverse-camera feed responsive while Overdrive records.

### ⚡ Optimizations & Fixes

- **More reliable DiLink 5 telemetry** — Improved speed, pedal, gear and indicator readings while keeping broader vehicle data available.
- **DiLink 5 cluster projection** — Added app and RoadSense map projection support, including ACC automations.
- **Safer compatibility** — Improved projection startup and cleanup, with every change limited to the selected DiLink 5 mode.
- **Fix swapped DiLink 5 cameras** — Camera Probe can switch camera order on affected models while Auto keeps existing defaults.
- **Faster camera acquisition (Di3)** — Parked-surveillance testing reduced worst-frame acquisition time from 31 ms to 19 ms (~39%) at the same recording FPS, without meaningful CPU or GPU regression.
