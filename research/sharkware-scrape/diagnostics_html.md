DIAGNOSTIC TOOLS

## Help your Shark find your Trigger.

If Sharkware cannot find your controller automatically, use the connection diagnostic to save its Bluetooth address and check the required helpers.

## Download the connection diagnostic

For existing Trigger owners. Enter your purchase email and the Sharkware licence associated with your Trigger access.

## Before you run it

*   Sharkware must already be installed. The tool stops if it is missing.
*   Park safely. Keep the Shark and controller powered, and connect the computer and Shark to the same Wi-Fi network.
*   Wireless ADB must already be enabled using the [Sharkware installation instructions](https://sharkwarehub.com/install.html).
*   Use an Android phone for the address-reading steps below. If you have an iPhone, you can borrow an Android phone.

Tested with our BYD AUTO Android 11 Shark and controller on Windows and Mac. Other older builds, different controllers and reboot reconnection across different vehicles remain unverified.

## Find your controller’s MAC address

1.   On an Android phone, install [BLE Hero by onceLabs](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.oncelabs.blehero).
2.   Allow its scanning permissions and scan near your powered controller. Find **DiKey**.
3.   Write down the MAC address immediately under DiKey. Use your own address, not the example shown here.
4.   **Turn Bluetooth off on the phone.** Leave Bluetooth on in the Shark.
5.   Extract the download and open **START_WINDOWS.cmd** or **START_MAC.command**. Follow the included START_HERE guide and enter the MAC address when asked.

If helpers were installed or replaced, keep phone Bluetooth off, unplug the controller and plug it back in. Confirm a button works, then test unplug/replug reconnection.

[Read the full illustrated guide](https://sharkwarehub.com/diagnostic-guide.html)

The diagnostic checks compatible helpers, creates a backup and retains your settings. It does not uninstall Sharkware or clear its data. If it stops, keep the error message and contact support.

Browser-install test for the BYD Shark. This is a separate test app, not a Sharkware update. Installation on the Shark is not yet confirmed.

1.   Park safely and open this page in the Shark’s built-in browser.

Test use only. The app requests no permissions and does not replace Sharkware, Control or the helpers. A successful download does not confirm that the vehicle permits installation.
