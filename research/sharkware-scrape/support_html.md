## Support Sharkware development

If Sharkware is useful to you, an optional contribution helps cover ongoing development, testing and hosting. Your existing licence and features stay yours whether you contribute or not. Thank you for your support.

All contributions are charged in AUD. Monthly and yearly support renew automatically. The custom amount is a one-off contribution from A$1. To cancel future renewals, email [support@sharkwarehub.com](mailto:support@sharkwarehub.com) from your payment email address.
