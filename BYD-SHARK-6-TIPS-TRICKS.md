## 1. Screen & Interface Shortcuts

### Quick Swipe Gestures
- **Swipe down from top**: Access quick settings panel (WiFi, Bluetooth, AVAS, brightness)
- **Swipe up from bottom**: See all open apps/windows
- **Swipe in from left/right edge**: Go back (same as back button)
- **Long press the screen rotation button**: Enter showroom/exhibition mode (hold 10 seconds to exit)

### Quick Settings Customization
1. Swipe down from top of screen
2. Press the "Customize" button
3. Access rarely-used shortcuts without permanently adding them to quick panel

### Split Screen
- Some BYD models support split-screen view for multitasking
- Check Settings > Display for split-screen options

## 2. Voice Commands & Wake Word

### Changing the Wake Word
You don't have to say "Hi BYD" - you can change it to anything!

**How to change:**
1. Tap the BYD logo (top left of screen)
2. Go to Voice Settings
3. Select "Wake Word" or "Custom Wake Word"
4. Type your preferred phrase

**Popular alternatives owners use:**
- "Hi Jarvis" (Marvel fans)
- "Hi HAL" (2001: A Space Odyssey)
- "Hi Twiki" (Buck Rogers reference - "BYD" sounds like "bidibidibidi")
- "Hey Shark" (for Shark owners)
- Any 2-4 syllable phrase

**Note:** "Hi BYD" still works even after changing the wake word, so be careful not to trigger it accidentally.

### Useful Voice Commands
Say your wake word, then:

**Climate Control:**
- "Set temperature to 22 degrees"
- "Turn on the AC"
- "Turn off the air conditioning"
- "Set fan speed to 3"

**Windows:**
- "Open all windows a little bit" (cracks all windows ~1cm - great for hot days!)
- "Close all windows"
- "Open driver window"
- "Roll down passenger window"

**Navigation:**
- "Navigate to [destination]"
- "Take me home"
- "Find nearest charging station"

**Media:**
- "Play music"
- "Next song"
- "Volume up/down"

**Vehicle:**
- "Open trunk"
- "Turn on seat heating"
- "Turn off seat cooling"

**Pro Tip:** You don't need to say the wake word for every command - after activating, it stays listening for follow-up commands.

## 3. Sound Customization

### AVAS (Pedestrian Warning Sound)

The low-speed pedestrian warning sound (AVAS) CAN be changed!

**How to change AVAS sound:**
1. Go to Settings > Sound
2. Look for "AVAS" or "Pedestrian Warning"
3. Choose between:
   - **Brand Sound** - BYD's signature sound
   - **Standard Sound** - Generic EV sound
   - Some regions have additional options

**Note:** AVAS cannot be completely disabled in many regions due to safety regulations. Thailand reportedly allows full disable.

### Lock/Unlock Sound - Transformer Noise?

Unfortunately, the **lock/unlock chirp sound is NOT user-customizable** through the standard interface on BYD vehicles. Here's what you can do:

**Built-in Options:**
- Enable/disable lock sound alert: Settings > Vehicle > Control Page > "Locking Sound Alert"
- Some models have horn confirmation option (single beep when locking)

**Workarounds for Custom Sounds:**
1. **External AVAS Device**: Companies like "Just Add Noise" (Australia) sell aftermarket EV sound emitters where you can upload custom sounds (including Transformer sounds!)
2. **Third-party horn module**: Some owners have installed programmable horn modules that play custom sounds

**What's NOT possible (without modification):**
- Uploading custom MP3 for lock/unlock sound
- Changing the lock chirp to a Transformer noise through software alone

### Notification/Chime Sounds
Some notification sounds CAN be changed:
1. Settings > Sound > Notifications
2. Browse available chime options
3. May vary by firmware version

## 4. Battery & Charging Tips

### LFP Battery Recalibration (IMPORTANT!)

BYD uses LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) batteries which require periodic recalibration.

**Why recalibrate:**
- Maintains accurate range estimates
- Keeps battery management system (BMS) calibrated
- Prevents "phantom" battery issues

**How to recalibrate (every 3 months):**
1. Drive until battery reaches ~10% or below
2. Charge to 100% in ONE session (don't interrupt)
3. Let it sit at 100% for at least 30 minutes before driving
4. Use AC charging for best results (not DC fast charging)

**Weekly habit:**
- Charge to 100% at least once per week
- This resets the coulomb counter for accurate SOC readings

### Daily Charging Best Practices
- LFP batteries are SAFE to charge to 100% regularly (unlike NMC batteries)
- You can ignore the "80% rule" for LFP batteries
- Avoid leaving at very low SOC (<10%) for extended periods
- Preconditioning before DC fast charging improves speeds

### Scheduled Charging
- Set charging to complete just before your departure time
- Battery performs better when warm from recent charging

## 5. Camp Mode & V2L Power

### Activating Camp Mode
Camp mode keeps climate control running while parked without draining auxiliary battery.

**How to activate (varies by model):**
1. Park and turn off the car
2. Look for Camp Mode in quick settings or climate menu
3. Set desired temperature
4. Vehicle stays on in low-power mode

**What stays active in Camp Mode:**
- Climate control (AC/heat)
- Interior lights
- USB power
- Infotainment (music, video)

### V2L (Vehicle-to-Load)

The Shark 6 can power external devices!

**V2L Specifications:**
- Output: Up to 3.6kW (varies by region)
- Voltage: 220-240V AC
- Can power: Laptops, camping gear, power tools, small appliances

**Using V2L:**
1. Locate V2L outlet (usually in bed or rear)
2. Plug in your device
3. Enable V2L in vehicle settings if required

**Camping power examples:**
- Electric kettle: 1,200W
- Portable fridge: 100-200W
- LED lights: 20-50W
- Phone chargers: 20-50W
- Electric stove: 1,000-1,500W
- Total capacity allows multiple devices simultaneously!

**Pro Tips:**
- Check if V2L works with car "off" - some BYDs require the car to be in accessory mode
- Monitor battery level to avoid being stranded
- Set minimum battery threshold in settings

## 6. Key Fob Tricks

### Hidden Key Fob Features
- **Long press lock**: Closes all windows (if equipped)
- **Long press unlock**: Opens all windows (if equipped)
- **Double press lock**: Enables/confirms lock with horn beep
- **Press lock + unlock simultaneously**: May trigger panic alarm or other function

### Remote Start
- Some models support remote climate pre-conditioning via key fob
- Hold specific button combination (check your manual)

### Digital Key (Phone as Key)
- BYD app allows phone to act as key
- Works via Bluetooth/NFC
- Can share temporary access with others

## 8. Driving Features

### Cruise Control Tips
**Adaptive Cruise (ACC/ICC):**
- Set following distance (usually 4 levels)
- Works best on highways
- Be aware of limitations in heavy traffic

**Speed Limit Recognition:**
- Camera reads speed signs
- Can auto-adjust cruise speed (if enabled)

### One-Pedal Driving
- Regenerative braking can be adjusted
- Higher regen = more one-pedal driving feel
- Lower regen = more traditional driving

### Snow/Eco/Sport Modes
Each mode adjusts:
- Throttle response
- Regenerative braking strength
- Traction control sensitivity
- Climate efficiency
